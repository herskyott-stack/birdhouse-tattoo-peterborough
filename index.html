The studio

Birdhouse Tattoo sits at 372 Water Street in the heart of downtown Peterborough. The studio works by appointment only — reach out on Instagram or by phone with your idea, and book your sitting from there.

Whether it's a first piece or the next one in a collection, an appointment-only studio means the chair time is yours: no walk-in rush, just a booked session focused on your tattoo.

Find the studio's latest work and updates on Instagram at @birdhouse_ptbo.
